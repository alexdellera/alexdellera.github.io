Biography

Alex Dell’Era is a strategic communication, public affairs and innovation professional specializing in the Life Sciences sector. For nearly two decades, he has worked across healthcare, contributing to communication strategies, stakeholder engagement and initiatives supporting digital transformation, with a particular focus on innovation governance, artificial intelligence and building trust among institutions, industry, healthcare professionals and society.

With an engineering background, he serves as Public Affairs & Communication Manager at Canon Medical Systems Italia and Coordinator of the FERPI Life Sciences Commission. In 2024, he was recognized among the Forbes Italy 100 Faces of Marketing and Communication. He is the author of numerous publications and articles on responsible innovation, digital health and strategic communication, promoting the adoption of emerging technologies through competence, transparency and long-term value for healthcare systems.
