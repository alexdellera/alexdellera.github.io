Biography

Alex Dell’Era is a strategic communication, public affairs and innovation professional specializing in the Life Sciences. For nearly two decades, he has worked in healthcare, contributing to communication strategies, stakeholder engagement and initiatives supporting digital transformation, with a particular focus on innovation governance, artificial intelligence and the development of trust among institutions, industry, healthcare professionals and citizens.

With an engineering background, he serves as Public Affairs & Communication Manager at Canon Medical Systems Italia and as Coordinator of the FERPI Life Sciences Commission. In 2024, he was included among the Forbes Italia 100 Faces of Marketing and Communication . He is the author of numerous articles and publications on responsible innovation, digital health and strategic communication, promoting the adoption of emerging technologies through expertise, transparency and long-term value for healthcare systems.
